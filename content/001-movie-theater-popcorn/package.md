Why does movie theater popcorn cost so much? Because the theater keeps about 81 cents of every snack-bar dollar, but only 42 to 49 cents of every ticket dollar. The rest of the ticket goes mostly to the studio.
But the snack bar isn't making theaters rich. After rent, staff and debt, Cinemark kept about 73 cents per visitor in 2025, and AMC lost money, mostly because of interest. Here's your $18.82 movie night, line by line.
