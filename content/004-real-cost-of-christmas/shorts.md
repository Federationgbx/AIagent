## Short 2 — Toys got 71% cheaper (officially)
- **Publish:** Thu 3 Dec 2026, 21:00 Germany
- **Hook text (frame 1, ≤ 7 words):** Toys got 71% cheaper. Officially.
- **Narration (≈ 105 words):** The US government's price index for toys was seventy-one percent lower in October twenty twenty-six than in October two thousand. Over the same years, prices overall rose ninety-four percent. In dollars: toys that cost a hundred dollars in two thousand would cost about twenty-nine today. Services, the things people do for you, about two hundred twenty-one. One caveat: price indexes adjust for quality, so part of that drop is better toys for the money. But in the UK, Canada and Australia, the official toy index is also lower than in two thousand. The stuff got cheaper. The people got expensive. Full breakdown on Pricewhy.

## Short 4 — An economist priced your unwanted gifts
- **Publish:** Mon 7 Dec 2026, 21:00 Germany
- **Hook text (frame 1, ≤ 7 words):** An economist priced your unwanted gifts
- **Narration (≈ 90 words):** In nineteen ninety-three, Yale economist Joel Waldfogel surveyed his students about their holiday gifts. His conclusion: holiday gift-giving destroys between ten percent and a third of the value of gifts. In his two surveys, a gift that cost a hundred dollars was worth about sixty-six to eighty-seven dollars to the person who got it. To be fair, later studies found the opposite: a hundred eleven, even a hundred thirty-five dollars of value per hundred spent. Sentimental value counts. So which side are you on? ## Short 5 — The Christmas bill arrives in January
